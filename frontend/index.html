BEL Secure Blockchain Architecture

BEL TrustChain — Secure Identity, Access Control & Digital Asset Platform

A tamper-proof decentralized framework combining self-sovereign Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs), Role & Clearance Access Control, and Cryptographic Digital Asset Tokenization with an immutable blockchain ledger. Developed by Team ShieldX.

Launch Platform Portal

🆔 Decentralized Identity (DID)

Eliminates single points of failure by assigning self-sovereign, cryptographically verifiable DIDs (e.g. did:bel:...) with strict clearance tiers (Levels 1 to 5).

🛡️ Role & Clearance Access Control

Enforces User Clearance ≥ Resource Requirement. All access attempts (allowed and denied) are permanently recorded on the blockchain for auditability.

📄 Digital Asset Tokens & Integrity

Digital assets and sensitive defense schematics are tokenized and directly linked to user DIDs with immutable SHA-256 anchors for instant tamper verification.
